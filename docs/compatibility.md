# Compatibility

| Android | Status |
|---|---|
| Android TV 12 (64-bit) | Developed and tested on emulators. |
| Android 5.0 to 11, 13 and later | Should work. |
| 32-bit and 64-bit Android TVs | One APK covers both. |

## Limits
- Dolby Vision, HDR and sound passthrough depend on the Android TV and what it is connected to.

If you try it, tell me how it went. Settings > About > Share logs gives a short code to paste in an issue.
